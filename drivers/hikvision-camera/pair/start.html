Add Hikvision Camera

Note: This is Phase 1 implementation. Manual camera addition will be enhanced with automatic discovery in future phases.

Step 1: Ensure NVR Connection

Make sure you have already added and configured your Hikvision NVR device. The camera will connect through the existing NVR connection.

Step 2: Manual Camera Addition

For now, cameras need to be added manually. In Phase 1, the basic camera structure is being established.

Future phases will include:

  • Automatic camera discovery through NVR
  • Camera selection interface
  • Live camera preview during pairing
